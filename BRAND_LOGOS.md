# No brand logos in the Folio

The Levni Folio does not accept brand logos or trademarks, and will not add them later. Requests for them are closed with a link to this page.

## Why

- **Law.** Most logos are protected by trademark or copyright and come with rules that forbid changing them. A Folio drawing is always changed: redrawn in the house line, recoloured by style packs. Shipping one would put you and the project at risk, and we cannot review every request for it.
- **Style.** A logo carries its own design. Redrawn as a riso pictogram it either stops looking like the brand or stops looking like Levni.
- **Upkeep.** One logo invites the next request. None at all is the only rule that ends the conversation.

## What this covers

- No prop is a logo, or reads as one: a bitten apple, a swoosh, a bird-shaped social icon, a real country's flag by accident.
- No prop is **named** after a brand. `npm test` blocks the words in [`library/brand-words.json`](library/brand-words.json) in names and aliases. Tags may still mention a brand, so a search for "tiktok" can find a generic phone.
- Generic objects are welcome: a phone, a play button, a speech bubble, a shopping cart.

## If you need logos

Use the brand's own press kit, or [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org/), which keeps marks exactly as brands publish them. A Levni scene can place your own SVG next to Folio drawings.
